TyPal keyboard: UI model

Built from the keyboard's own source: the layout maths in KeyboardLayout, the measurements in KeyboardChrome, the colours in KeyboardTheme, and each panel's SwiftUI view. One CSS pixel is one iOS point. The first keyboard is live; every state below is the same renderer.

Feature demos

Typing

Try it yourself

  • Tap keys: the first key press rolls the five places away and shows your emoji; the pill beside the language switch brings them back.
  • Tap the five-way toggle to open Chat, AI, Translate and the clipboard.
  • In AI, tap a highlighted private detail to see its code, pick a style from the menu beside the model, tap an action, or tap Ask TyPal… to type an ask (the keys come back under a 210 pt strip).
  • Tap the logo for one-handed typing, and the shield for what TyPal keeps private.
  • Tap in the text field to move the cursor. Typing goes in there; opening AI or Translate walks the cursor to both ends and back, and reads the whole message.
  • Home Screen shows the iPhone's Home Screen; tap TyPal's icon there and the app opens out of it.
  • Tap any state below to load it here.

What is exact, and what is redrawn

  • Exact from the code: every height and width, key positions, corner radii, fonts and sizes, theme colours, shadows, panel contents and copy, and the Sinhala phonetic typing.
  • The icons: the same SF Symbols the keyboard asks for by name, as outlines read from the system, in regular, medium and semibold.
  • The iPhone: a 393 × 852 pt screen in a drawn frame, with the status bar and Dynamic Island; the app behind the keyboard is a stand-in.
  • Drawn by iOS, approximated here: the segmented controls' selected capsule, menus, the list swipe actions, and the bar under the keyboard.
  • Animations: the timings are the keyboard's (0.28 s place switch, 0.46 s top-bar morph); the morph's icon choreography is simplified.
  • The hand: not part of the keyboard. It is there in the demos to show what is being tapped, and goes away when you tap the phone yourself.
  • Sample content: names, messages, AI answers, translations, model and style names are examples.
